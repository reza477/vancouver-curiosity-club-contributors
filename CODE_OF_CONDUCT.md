# Community code of conduct

Vancouver Curiosity Club is built for curiosity, thoughtful conversation, and welcoming participation. The same standard applies to this repository.

## Our standards

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate with patience, clarity, and respect;
- welcome different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience;
- offer specific, constructive feedback;
- respect privacy, consent, intellectual property, and community boundaries;
- focus disagreements on ideas and evidence, not people;
- stop behavior when someone says it is unwelcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository activity and to public spaces where someone is representing Vancouver Curiosity Club.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
