# Phase 6 known limitations

- The Phase 6 candidate is not deployed. Hosted D1/R2 upload, preview,
  publication, cleanup, and byte-serving behavior therefore remains externally
  unverified until a later explicitly authorized owner-only deployment.
- The live owner-only deployment remains version 8. It does not contain the
  Phase 3–6 organizer, conflict, publication, CMS, or media workflows.
- Sites provides no promised cron or realtime subscription. Scheduled event
  publication and official Meetup refresh remain request-driven, bounded, and
  honestly visible through refresh/redirect states. Maintenance is separated
  from a full render to stay inside D1's per-invocation statement ceiling.
- Access remains exactly one allowed owner and zero groups. Hosted
  second-identity Organizer/Administrator testing is not possible without a
  separate access-policy authorization.
- Phase 6 accepts JPEG, PNG, and WebP images only. It does not accept SVG, GIF,
  PDF, video, audio, archives, or remote-URL imports.
- No real photographs, replacement logo, or replacement Open Graph artwork
  have been supplied with confirmed rights, credit, and participant-consent
  metadata. Existing local Field Notes artwork remains the public fallback.
- No legal name, legal form/status wording, registration number, effective
  date, charity confirmation, or approved legal footer has been supplied.
  Neutral community-organization language remains public.
- The exact Meetup discussion URL, public contact address, approved organizer
  biographies, Resources/reading-packet content, verified accessibility facts,
  and complete final public copy remain missing. Off-Radar Eats and
  Contemplative Meditation + Journaling Circle remain unpublished pending
  sufficient confirmed content and links.
- Editor role — **Not implemented — authorized cut**.
- Viewer role — **Not implemented — authorized cut**.
- Realtime subscriptions — **Not implemented — authorized cut**.
  General CMS/media management remains Owner/Administrator-only.
- Approved-real-artwork browser smoke — **Awaiting owner**. Synthetic local
  non-person artwork verifies the mechanics only; it does not establish
  rights, credit, consent, or approval for a real public asset.
- The CMS is a bounded structured editor, not an arbitrary page builder. It
  accepts only the shipped block types, vetted typography choices, validated
  palette fields, safe internal routes, and confirmed HTTPS destinations.
- Stable public media routes use bounded revalidation and a live published-
  usage check. They cannot provide permanent immutable-cache semantics because
  rights, consent, publication, and deletion authority remain revocable.
- Public previews are authenticated and intentionally non-shareable. There is
  no public preview token or anonymous draft-review workflow.
- Imports, exports, backup UI, CSV/ICS workflows, public forms, and submissions
  remain Phase 7. Email, attendee accounts, internal RSVP, payments, comments,
  messaging, forums, chat, and automatic Meetup write-back are not implemented.
- Phase 7 has not started, and the Phase 6 candidate must not be deployed or
  used to widen the one-owner/zero-group access policy in this phase.
