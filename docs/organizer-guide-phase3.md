# Phase 3 organizer guide

This guide applies after a Phase 3-or-later version is explicitly deployed and
your matching ChatGPT identity has accepted an active invitation.

## What you can do

- View the organization-wide private Calendar in Agenda, Day, Week, or Month
  view.
- Search and filter by organizer, club, status, lane, category, source, and
  date.
- Find unscheduled Ideas in the separate Ideas area.
- Create an Idea or Draft in a club assigned to you.
- Edit a private Idea or Draft that you own or co-organize.
- Read source-controlled Meetup and existing reserving/public records without
  changing them.
- View Team display names, roles, and club assignments; club and team
  management remain role-gated.
- Read assigned-club planning details and Meetup feed health without seeing
  saved feed addresses or refresh controls.
- Use the private notification center and mark relevant items read or unread.
- Update your display name, initials, accessible calendar color, private
  biography draft, attribution consent, calendar preference, and in-app
  notification preference.

## Creating an Idea or Draft

The event form is one page. Required scheduling rules are:

- an Idea can be unscheduled, timed, or all-day;
- a Draft must be timed or all-day;
- timed end must be after start;
- all-day end is an exclusive calendar date;
- the default timezone is `America/Vancouver`, but the saved record keeps its
  actual IANA timezone.

You can add private notes and draft public-facing copy. Those fields remain
private in Phase 3. A deliberately supplied real Meetup event URL is only a
reference on a manual record; it does not make the website a Meetup source or
enable write-back.

## Changes and refresh

The workspace refreshes through ordinary page reloads and explicit actions.
It does not claim realtime behavior.

If an edit is stale, refresh the record and reconcile your changes. The system
does not silently overwrite the newer version. Soft-deleted records can be
restored; there is no permanent-delete or cancellation control in Phase 3.

## Privacy

Organizer pages and APIs are private, no-store, and noindex. Do not place
secrets, invitation links, feed addresses, or sensitive identity data in event
notes. Public pages cannot read Phase 3 Ideas, Drafts, private notes,
notifications, invitations, activity history, or organizer email.
